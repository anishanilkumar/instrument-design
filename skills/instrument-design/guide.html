Instrument01 · Principles
Where it comes from

The Iron Man HUD, built the way real optics are built.

The brief was the Iron Man HUD, and the design commits to it. What it refuses is the usual execution: hairline strokes, hex grids, drifting particles, glow and decorative 9px text. On a screen that is lit all day and read from across a room, those disappear or exhaust. The MCU HUDs are precision optics: engraved arcs, index ticks, machined bezels. It sits closer to an aviation chronograph than to a screensaver.

  1. The display never lies.

    Data that may be old looks old: it loses its colour and stops counting, and never keeps counting down on numbers it can't refresh. "Nothing planned" beats a confident wrong answer.

  2. Motion means something changed.

    In its resting state nothing moves, except at most one purposeful indicator. Things animate when they change, not for atmosphere. Reduced-motion is always respected.

  3. Every colour has a job.

    Cool arc-blue is live, warm gold is attention, red is the one truly bad state. Rare colours stay rare, so they read when they appear.

  4. Legibility over decoration.

    There is a hard minimum type size. A label nobody can read is decoration pretending to be information.

  5. Brackets, not boxes.

    A surface is a raised panel with two engraved corner brackets, not a rounded, bordered, shadowed card.

  6. Offline is a designed state.

    Fonts are self-hosted. Losing the connection desaturates the interface to slate, like an instrument on backup power, not a broken web page.

  7. Decisions are written down.

    Stylesheets explain why: the measured number, the alternative that was tried and rejected. That is part of the style.

Instrument02 · Colour
Palette

Dark, never black. Two accent temperatures.

There is no light theme. The ground is a deep anodized navy-slate, because pure black on an LCD reads as a dead panel rather than a dark one. The identity is in the pairing: Iron Man is gold and arc-blue, and two hues let "important" be told apart from "live".

Adding a colour

Allowed when a project needs a meaning the palette doesn't have. Pick it at the arc's brightness so it belongs to the family, give it a semantic alias, and note why in a comment. Recipie's cook asked for a traffic light, so it added go #4FD68A as ready, with gold as almost and alert as no.

Offline

The tokens are swapped wholesale on a root attribute. Arc becomes slate, gold becomes #8A7350, ink softens to #B9C4D1. No component knows it is drawing an offline state.

Instrument04 · Structure & components
The instrument

Two brackets and a 2px rule.

Tonight
12
recipes ready to cook

The panel face is panel, with an engraved bracket in the top-left and bottom-right corners only. That's the machined bezel instead of a card.

Rules never drop below 2px. Hairlines smear or vanish, and the engraved look lives entirely in them. Radius is 0 or 2px. Nothing soft: no shadows, glows, blur or visible gradients.

Touch targets are at least 44px. Page content sits in a 54rem column.

Instrument05 · Motion & states
Motion

Still until something happens.

Easing

cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)

Quick out, long settle. 200–520ms.

Attention

steps(2)

A mechanical blink, never an elastic bounce or soft pulse.

Change

rotate & fade

No scrolling tickers. New content arrives once and settles.

Instrument06 · Identity, voice, charts & checklist
App icon

Full-bleed ground so the OS mask only ever cuts dark. One arc-blue line mark in the central 80%, with an optional engraved rule under it at 35%. Line icons use a 20-unit grid, 1.6 stroke, round caps, no fill.

Voice

Terse, instrument-like, honest. Short uppercase nouns for labels. If a number might be wrong, say how old it is.

Starting a project
  1. Copy the starter stylesheet and the fonts folder, licences included.
  2. Head the stylesheet with its lineage and any deliberate departures.
  3. Name semantic aliases for the project's states; justify new colours.
  4. Design offline, stale, expired, empty and loading states.
  5. Icon, theme-color #0A0E14, manifest.
  6. Run lint.sh and fix every failure.
  7. Look at it at 375px, offline, and with reduced motion on.

Charts

The number first. Colour keeps its meaning.

Arc is the live or primary series and gold the warm one. Today is arc, the other days arc at rest. A dashed gold line is a plan or target; a dashed outline is a day not yet here.

Filled bars and areas use the accents one step deeper (#249CB8, #C2842B) when two series sit side by side. Lines, ticks and text keep the full tokens.

Gridlines are the only hairline in the system. Axis and legend text never goes below the floor.
